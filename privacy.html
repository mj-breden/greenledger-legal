GreenLedger

Privacy Policy

GreenLedger is a private application operated by one individual for their own household. It does not collect data from the public.

What the Google Drive access is for

The app asks for the drive.file permission so it can upload backup copies of its own database and plant photos to a folder it creates in the owner's Google Drive. It can only see files it created itself. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.

What is stored and where

Plant records and photos are stored on the owner's own computer and in that backup folder. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Plant photos may be sent to the Anthropic API to identify the plant, as described in Anthropic's own privacy policy.

Deleting data

The owner can delete the backup folder at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
